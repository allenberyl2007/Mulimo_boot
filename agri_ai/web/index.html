      How the scores and indicators are calculated

      Every strategy uses the same historical weather, reference soil, slope and assumed management. Only the crop sequence changes. Indicator scores are scaled relative to the three strategies in this comparison.

      • Soil carbon / organic matter, erosion protection and nitrogen fixation: each has its own user-selected priority weight.
      • Water: 75% lower simulated water use and 25% lower simulated crop water stress.
      • Food energy: estimated crop calories per harvest, not a measure of nutrition, income or food security.
      • Final score: five indicator scores weighted by the priorities you selected. It only compares these scenarios.
      • The displayed 10th–90th percentile range reflects three different pest-seed simulations; it is not a statistical confidence interval.

      Indicator guide: soil-carbon change estimates organic carbon change over the three simulated crops; erosion estimates modeled soil loss; nitrogen fixation is the twin's estimate for legume crops; disease loss is a modeled crop-loss estimate.
